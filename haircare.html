Application Protocol

How to Maximize Results

STEP 01

Warm Water Saturation

Soak hair completely with lukewarm water for 60 seconds to open cuticles and loosen surface residue.

STEP 02

Two-Minute Contact

Lather 2 pumps into scalp using fingertips. Allow the curd protein to penetrate for a full two minutes before rinsing.

STEP 03

Cool Water Seal

Finish with a cool water rinse to lock down the hair cuticle for maximum light reflection and natural silkiness.
