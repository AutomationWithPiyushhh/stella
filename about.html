Our Origin

Where Botanical Science Meets Dermatology

The Stella was founded to answer a persistent gap in personal care: formulas that were either too harsh with stripped chemical actives or overly fragrant herbal solutions lacking clinical efficacy.

We developed a balanced middle ground: honest percentages of dermatological gold standards like Tranexamic Acid, Niacinamide, and Salicylic Acid paired with native nourishment like bio-fermented curd proteins, raw shea butter, and licorice lipid extracts.
